Before you begin

Good to know.

A few practical details, so you know what to expect.

Can I start transcribing on this website?

This is the introduction to WorkerWhisper. To transcribe, set up your own workspace using the GitHub guide. You’ll need a Cloudflare account and some comfort following technical setup instructions. There’s no shared hosted service.

What happens to my audio?

Your computer prepares the recording and sends small pieces to Cloudflare for transcription. WorkerWhisper saves the finished text, but does not store the uploaded audio in its transcript archive. Audio processing happens at Cloudflare, even when you run the app locally.

Which recordings and languages can I use?

Start with an Ogg or Opus file. Language is detected automatically, with guidance for English, Traditional Chinese, Thai, and Filipino. Results depend on the recording; review important names, quotes, and mixed-language passages before relying on them.

Does it cost anything?

The project is free to use under the MIT licence. You provide your own Cloudflare account and pay for its usage. Transcription can incur charges, and the daily app allowance does not limit every hosting charge. Follow the setup guide to protect your workspace before putting it online.

What if a recording takes a while?

Progress and time estimates help you follow longer recordings. Speed varies with your connection, audio, and Cloudflare’s processing load. Keep the browser page open while working; refreshing or closing it loses unfinished progress.
